## 🕹️ Overview

Parsec streams your gaming PC to any device over the internet with sub-20ms latency, enabling remote play, couch co-op over the web, and team collaboration.

Whether you're a newcomer or a veteran, Parsec provides the tools you need to get the most out of your gaming tools experience. Designed for Windows and macOS, it delivers a seamless, high-performance workflow.
